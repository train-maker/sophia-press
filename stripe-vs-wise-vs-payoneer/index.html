Stripe vs Wise vs Payoneer — receiving international B2B payments.

Updated 2026-05-02 · 7-minute read · written by Sophia Press

Disclosure. Some links below are affiliate / partner links. Sophia uses Stripe to bill its own products. We disclose those facts and let the editorial ranking stand on its own.

An SMB exporter who lands an international buyer needs to get paid. The friction at this last step kills more deals than any other part of the funnel. Three platforms cover 80%+ of the market in 2026: Stripe, Wise, and Payoneer. Each is built for a different shape of buyer.

The TL;DR

1. Stripe — the right choice for SMB exporters selling productized services or digital goods

Best for: SMB exporters with productized offerings (PDFs, digital products, fixed-price services, recurring subscriptions) accepting card payments globally.

Stripe is the cleanest infrastructure for productized payments. Payment Links work without a website. Recurring subscriptions handle dunning automatically. Coverage is 47 countries for direct payouts, with most card networks accepted globally.

What works: the cleanest developer experience, payment links you can create in 60 seconds, recurring billing that handles failed cards correctly. If your B2B is a productized SaaS, Concierge service, PDF guide, or any product with a fixed price tag, Stripe wins.

What doesn't: 2.9% + 30¢ on card payments is meaningfully more expensive than Wise for large invoice amounts. For a $50,000 wire from a customer who can pay by bank transfer, paying $1,450 to Stripe is wasteful — use Wise.

Sophia uses Stripe for all six of our productized SKUs ($9 to $199/mo). The integration is one-click, the dashboard is the cleanest in fintech, and the payment-link product means you can sell a digital product before you have a website ready.

2. Wise Business — the right choice for SMB exporters invoicing larger B2B deals via bank transfer

Best for: SMB exporters whose buyers pay by international bank transfer at $1,000+/invoice, especially across multi-currency lanes.

Wise Business gives you local-currency receiving accounts in USD, EUR, GBP, AUD, NZD, SGD, HUF, RON, TRY — your buyer in any of those countries can pay you with a domestic bank transfer (no SWIFT, no correspondent fees) and you receive in their currency, then convert at the mid-market rate when you choose.

What works: the cheapest international FX in the review. Transparent fees published per corridor. Multi-currency holding lets you wait for favorable conversion timing.

What doesn't: not built for card payments. Not built for recurring subscriptions. If your customer wants to pay with a credit card, Wise isn't the right tool.

Start with Wise Business →

3. Payoneer — the right choice for marketplace sellers and freelancer exporters

Best for: SMB exporters earning via Amazon, Upwork, Fiverr, or other large marketplaces — especially in countries where local banking is friction-heavy.

Payoneer built its product around marketplace payouts. If 60% of your revenue comes from Amazon Seller Central, Upwork, Fiverr, or similar — Payoneer's integration depth and country coverage matters. They're licensed in markets where Stripe isn't, including parts of MENA, sub-Saharan Africa, South Asia.

What works: country coverage that exceeds Stripe and Wise in some regions. Marketplace integrations are real. Receiving accounts in 9 currencies.

What doesn't: fees are higher than Wise for pure FX. UX is worse than Stripe for productized billing. If you don't sell on a marketplace, the marketplace integration value is zero.

The honest stack for most SMB exporters

You probably want all three, used for what each does best:

  1. Stripe for any productized product (digital, subscription, fixed-price service) — buyers pay with cards, you get clean dashboards.
  2. Wise Business for invoiced B2B deals over $1,000 — buyers pay by local bank transfer, you save 2-5% vs Stripe on FX.
  3. Payoneer only if you sell on Amazon / Upwork / Fiverr — otherwise skip.

Setup time for all three combined: ~3 hours. Annual cost on a $10K/month business: under $50 in subscription fees (most charge nothing — they take per-transaction fees only).

For SMB exporters reading this who haven't set up payments yet: start with Stripe for productized billing. Add Wise Business once you have invoiced deals over $1,000. Add Payoneer only if you're a marketplace seller. Skip the payment platforms that aren't on this list — most are second-tier resellers of the rails the three above own.
