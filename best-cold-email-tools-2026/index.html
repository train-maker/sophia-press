The 2026 reality you can't skip

None of these tools matter if your sending domain isn't authenticated. Per Google and Microsoft's 2024-2025 enforcement, unauthenticated mail is throttled before any human ever sees it. Required:

  1. SPF — list IPs allowed to send mail from your domain
  2. DKIM — cryptographic signature on every outgoing message
  3. DMARC — tells receivers what to do if SPF/DKIM fail

Five minutes of dig commands to verify. All four tools above include guides, but the records have to live on your domain — not on the tool.
